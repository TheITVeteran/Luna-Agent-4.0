Expression (face)

Three folders: idle body clips in the animations root, talking in talking/, expression face VRMA in expressions/. While silent, idle body and expression alternate on one timer. During TTS, expression VRMA is off and one talking body clip loops until the response finishes (no mid-speech talk swap). Legacy: data/vrm/animations/. FBX retargets in-browser. Low-motion body .vrma can be hidden server-side (LUNA_VRMA_MIN_MOTION_RAD=0 lists all).

Speech
